The paper jury

Enter the opened scores. The same ledger as the live game grades the room.

One juror per line: Ada 0.62 or Ada 62. Use Ada recuse to record a recusal. A score of 1 means 1.00. Scores are rounded to the nearest hundredth.

Everyone named in any entered round is treated as seated for every entered round. An omitted name forfeits their ante when at least one score opens. Leave a whole round blank to skip it.
